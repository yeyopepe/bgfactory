# Naturalist Post-Apocalypse Monochrome Style Guide

## Style Overview

The visual identity is inspired by 19th century naturalist field journals, scientific expedition notebooks, botanical atlases, zoological studies, and hand-colored engravings. The objective is not to depict a brutal post-apocalyptic wasteland, but to document a transformed world where nature has reclaimed the remnants of human civilization.

The artwork should feel as though it was created by an explorer, scientist, or cartographer carefully recording observations in the field.

The overall tone is contemplative, documentary, melancholic, and curious rather than dramatic, heroic, or action-oriented.

---

## Core Artistic Principles

### Drawing First, Color Second

Every illustration must be built around strong draftsmanship.

The drawing should remain visually compelling even if all color is removed.

The image relies primarily on:

- Ink linework
- Graphite textures
- Fine hatching and cross-hatching
- Grey washes
- Paper texture
- Value contrast

Color is never responsible for carrying the composition.

---

## Monochromatic Foundation

Approximately 85-95% of the image should exist in black, white, and grey tones.

Preferred materials and visual references:

- Graphite pencil
- Technical pen
- Fountain pen illustration
- Charcoal accents
- Diluted black ink washes
- Antique scientific prints

Greys should be rich and varied rather than flat.

Use:

- Warm greys
- Cool greys
- Deep blacks
- Soft paper whites

Avoid high saturation and full-color rendering.

---

## Selective Watercolor Accents

Color must be rare, intentional, and meaningful.

Watercolor should appear as if manually applied after the drawing was completed.

Characteristics:

- Transparent pigment
- Soft gradients
- Irregular edges
- Natural pigment pooling
- Occasional watercolor blooms
- Visible paper interaction

The watercolor should never dominate the illustration.

Its purpose is to guide attention and create emotional resonance.

---

## Restricted Color Palette

Use only a small number of recurring colors throughout the project.

### Sage Green

Meaning:

- Life
- Regrowth
- Ecological recovery

### Ochre Gold

Meaning:

- Discovery
- Memory
- Traces of civilization

### Petroleum Blue

Meaning:

- Water
- Mystery
- Forgotten technology

### Rust Red

Meaning:

- Industrial remains
- Hazard
- Decay

Most illustrations should contain only one or two accent colors.

---

## Subject Treatment

### Environment

Nature is the protagonist.

Human structures exist as evidence of the past rather than the focus of the image.

Examples:

- Buildings consumed by vegetation
- Abandoned infrastructure integrated into ecosystems
- Flooded streets becoming wetlands
- Forests reclaiming industrial areas

### Flora and Fauna

Approach all living things with scientific curiosity.

Subjects should be observed and documented rather than dramatized.

Visual inspiration should come from:

- Botanical plates
- Zoological studies
- Scientific observation journals

---

## Composition

Favored compositions:

- Observational
- Balanced
- Informative
- Naturalistic

The viewer should feel like they are studying a careful field observation.

Avoid:

- Extreme action poses
- Cinematic explosions
- Excessive motion blur
- Superhero framing
- Epic fantasy compositions

---

## Texture and Surface

All artwork should preserve the appearance of traditional media.

Important elements:

- Visible paper grain
- Ink imperfections
- Pencil texture
- Watercolor transparency
- Organic brush marks

The image should never look overly polished or digitally rendered.

---

## Emotional Tone

Primary emotions:

- Wonder
- Discovery
- Solitude
- Reflection
- Quiet resilience

The world is damaged but alive.

The illustrations should communicate that something beautiful emerged after the collapse.

---

## Negative Style Guidance

Avoid:

- Comic-book aesthetics
- Anime influences
- Hyper-saturated colors
- Modern concept art rendering
- Heavy digital painting effects
- Cyberpunk aesthetics
- Neon palettes
- Photobashing appearance
- 3D-rendered look
- High fantasy visual language

---
